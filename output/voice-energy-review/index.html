The Moonlight Picnic · Performance direction 02

Finding the right energy.

Eight short comparisons with Tamsin as Pip and Mike as Bear.

The new brief: animated children’s-presenter energy at roughly 70% intensity. A clear speaking voice, playful emphasis and engaged questions, with time to think between instructions.

Listen for personality and clear speech.
Invitations and discoveries can sparkle. Instructions stay steady and encouraging. The ending relaxes without becoming a whisper. Start with Bear’s welcome and Pip’s discovery.
