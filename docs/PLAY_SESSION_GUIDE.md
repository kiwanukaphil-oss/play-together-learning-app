# Watching a play session — parent guide

One relaxed session tells us more than any amount of planning. Aim for 10–15 minutes, whenever your child is fresh and happy to play. Stop the moment they want to stop.

## Before

- Use the laptop and speakers they'll normally use. Volume at a comfortable level.
- Have paper and a pencil nearby for the lantern drawing.
- Open the [live app](https://kiwanukaphil-oss.github.io/play-together-learning-app/). If they've played before and you want a fresh start, finish the current picnic and choose **Play another picnic**.

## During

- Say only: "This is a game with Pip and Bear. Have a go." Then **sit slightly behind and stay quiet**. The most useful moments are when they're unsure. Wait about 10 seconds before helping.
- If they ask you something, it's fine to answer, but note what they asked.
- Don't praise or correct the answers yourself. Let the game do it, so we see whether its feedback works.

## What to notice

Jot down quick notes. Short phrases are perfect.

1. **Getting started:** Did they understand what to do after Bear's welcome? Did they watch the examples or skip them?
2. **Listening:** Did they use **Hear the word ♫**? Could they hear the letter sounds in the word?
3. **Stuck moments:** Where did they hesitate, click randomly, or look at you? Did they find **Help me**?
4. **Mistakes:** How did they react to "not quite yet"? Upset, unbothered, or trying again?
5. **Voices and sounds:** Did they react to Pip and Bear? Did they copy them, laugh, or ignore them? Were any sounds startling or annoying?
6. **The drawing:** Did they actually draw on paper, or skip it?
7. **Mouse and keyboard:** Anything awkward? Small targets, or trouble clicking?
8. **The ending:** Did they want to play again or choose a decoration? What did they say?

## After

- Ask one question: "What was your favourite bit?" Note the answer in their words.
- Send Claude a short summary of what you noticed: what worked, where they got stuck, and anything surprising.

## Privacy

Keep your notes **private**: on paper, or in a file outside the project folder. Don't save photos, recordings or your child's name in the project. Only the anonymised product issues and fixes go into the roadmap.
